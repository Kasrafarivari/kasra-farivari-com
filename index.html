About

I'm a product manager currently at Yahoo, previously at Product Madness and Big Fish Games, and an MBA out of Georgetown. That's the resume version. This page is the rest of it.

I grew up moving between cultures and languages, which I think is part of why I ended up in product management: it's a job that's mostly translation — between engineers and executives, between what customers say and what they mean, between a spec and something a person actually wants to use. I like the parts of the job that are unglamorous: sitting with a confusing support ticket until it makes sense, or rewriting the same paragraph of a PRD four times until it's actually clear.

Outside of work, I'm a genuinely serious video gamer — not a "I play games to unwind" gamer, a person who has strong, specific opinions about patch notes gamer. I write occasionally, mostly to think out loud. And I try to answer my own email, even the boring ones.

Currently

  • Working on consumer product growth at Yahoo, with a focus on where AI actually helps versus where it's just noise.
  • Playing through a long backlog of RPGs I never finished in my twenties.
  • Reading more than I have in years — mostly nonfiction, some Persian poetry in translation.
  • Slowly rebuilding a home lab setup, mostly as an excuse to learn things outside of product work.

Interests

Games

I've played seriously since childhood — strategy and RPGs especially. I pay attention to design decisions the way some people watch film for the editing: pacing, difficulty curves, the small choices that make a system feel fair or unfair.

Writing

I write short pieces, mostly about product craft and occasionally about nothing in particular. It's less about publishing and more about figuring out what I actually think.

Language and culture

I grew up around Persian language and culture and still care about staying connected to it — food, poetry, and keeping up with family traditions, wherever I happen to be living.
